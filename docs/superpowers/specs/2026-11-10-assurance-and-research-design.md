## Intent and source hierarchy

Make the existing institution and research pipeline robust to the audit's demonstrated failures, preserve reproducible historical interpretations, and prepare a separately reviewed experiment on useful supervisor decisions. Apply the owner-wide workstation policy, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, and accepted ADRs. Current `docs/GATES.md` and observed build receipts supersede the dated status language in historical plans, reports and ADR validation sections; accepted decisions themselves are not silently modified.

The maintained `docs/report/latest.md` and `latest.pdf` synthesize three distinct evidence sets: scripted mechanisms; an archived, two-model swarm study reconstructed using fresh synthetic signatures; and a one-model, 160-call paired supervisor pilot. The last reports 18 invalid raw approvals and 40 incorrect explicit denials, with 30 valid local effects. These are observations in that cohort, not a general rate or proof of improved model judgment. `docs/report/CORRECTIONS-2026-11-09.md`, `data/README.md`, `docs/SWARM.md`, and the pilot record establish provenance, incompleteness and separate denominators. Existing AI review records are model outputs, not independent human acceptance.

The institution checks integrity, policy and seated-auditor attestations but delegates semantic safety to auditors. The baseline/pilot controller separately checks the exact synthetic issuer/claim/scope/artifact/run contract. Do not conflate these mechanisms, modify historical output, or make model-visible verifier data an execution credential. Effects remain local committed SQLite rows.

## Alternatives and recommendation

1. **Recommended: staged assurance, then a versioned research interface.** Repair five bounded audit findings under existing contracts, then specify and test an offline verifier-result intervention with a new schema and fresh held-out design. Benefits: separately reviewable fixes, preserved study comparability, and a concrete route to investigating blanket denial. Cost: a new model study still requires qualification and its own design and evidence.
2. **Security fixes only.** Smaller immediate scope, but leaves the paper's most useful next research question unprepared. Suitable if continuation of model research is deferred.
3. **New study or platform first.** Deferred: reproducing an unsafe parser, misleading checker status, or unprotected local artifacts would weaken the evidence; a public service, external effect, or new provider adds unrelated authority and risk.

The recommended ordering is a planning judgment, not a claim that the human team selected it. Deliver the immediate work in independently reviewable tasks; prepare, but do not activate, subsequent research and infrastructure lanes.

## Required behavior

1. Malformed untrusted institution attestations cannot crash execution or poison an otherwise valid case. Check names/types before dictionary lookup and validate signature representation before constant-time comparison. Preserve distinct-seated-quorum, binding, transaction/audit and case semantics; valid submissions must still count. Bound and document admission behavior; maintain compatibility with malformed records already in a database. Apply the same fail-closed discipline to malformed evidence signatures that can reach the checker.
2. The checker CLI must exit nonzero on either a base-kernel violation or an expected mutant escaping detection, with a machine-readable outcome for automation. A reproduction run must record and propagate checker failure, retaining failure evidence. Preserve existing text output or offer an explicit versioned mode; `--skip-checker` remains an explicit recorded omission. No result implies an unbounded proof.
3. New swarm votes and attestations use a closed, bounded, explicitly versioned parsing treatment. Malformed, ambiguous, extra-key, duplicate-key, non-JSON, string-boolean and negated prose must never become approval or signing. Preserve the historical parser and immutable raw/derived records; existing archive reconstruction uses recorded outcomes and must not be silently reinterpreted. Persist parser identity before calls and in completed new-run provenance. Analysis must reject mixed or unknown treatments by default or explicitly stratify them with separate denominators; only identified historical inputs may be mapped to the legacy treatment. The live supervisor's separate closed schema stays unchanged.
4. New swarm runs use private output roots and files, independent of ambient umask. Protect sealed truth, model reasons, private logs and SQLite files; refuse unsafe pre-existing, symlinked, externally owned or permissive paths rather than changing their permissions. Separate any later sanitized public export from private detector material. User/host compromise remains outside this filesystem boundary; do not imply cryptographic sealing.
5. Bounded exploration closes all database connections on normal, duplicate-state, depth-limit, violation and exception paths, including queued states on early return. Confirm invariants and mutant behavior remain equivalent; measure warnings, elapsed time and memory against the configured worker limit rather than assume a fixed reduction.
6. Preserve the frozen pilot. A new, opt-in, versioned verifier-result protocol is a separate information intervention: an independently constructed snapshot must bind the verifier's result to the exact evidence, artifact, run and snapshot, represent verified/invalid/unknown without leaking keys or oracle labels, and fail closed on absent, stale, substituted, forged or mismatched results. Model output remains an untrusted recommendation; execution independently rechecks current policy and receipts transactionally. Define the exact wire format, issuer trust and expiry in a successor design/ADR as required before implementation. Do not reuse the previous cohort's design hash, results, authorization or schema.

## Research and operations follow-up

- The new held-out study must freeze treatment, model/route, randomization, candidate and calibration separation, honest/invalid/adversarial cases, sample rationale, stopping, exclusions, attrition, and paired analysis before collection. Report valid-case acceptance and unsafe approvals jointly, plus bad denials, invalid outputs, dispatch failures, complete pairs, authorization denials and effects. Include uncertain sampling assumptions and no post hoc substitution. A synthetic/fake rehearsal cannot establish a model effect.
- Later research can examine correlated honest errors, adaptive or seat-steering adversaries, larger/multiple-case checking, and protocol composition. Verify source assumptions before claiming Byzantine fault tolerance, public-key guarantees or independent errors. Do not infer natural-world collusion from assigned cells or detector text similarity. A stronger cryptographic institution, external actions/outbox, public sensor and autonomous dispatch require separate designs and authorization.
- Before any paid request, meet ADR-0010's source, account, billing, model, TLS, handling, calibration, single-use ledger and reconciliation requirements; existing budget authorization does not supply missing facts or authorize a new cohort automatically. Before cloud operation, refresh service/expiry, inventory, IAM, costs, host/collector acceptance and recovery evidence. Before heavy builds or reproduction use `workstation-storage` admission for the actual output. Preserve all WIP, historical artifacts, receipts, data and rollback sets.
- Current 11-lane CI results are source-bound to the commit tested, not future changes. Recheck latest-stable/upcoming/previous versions and exact artifacts before upgrade; keep promised consumers and the accepted rollback set. CI configuration is not execution.

## Evidence and limitations of this planning review

The review inspected the maintained paper and extractable PDF, earlier report and correction, scripted draft, paired-pilot study and design, current architecture/ADRs/gates/roadmap, security/operations/testing/provider documents, and relevant historic plans and review summaries. It inventoried 80 tracked Markdown files; historical plans and large build/operations logs were navigated by section and relevance rather than represented as a fresh line-by-line audit. The 32-page `Swarm Alignment Hackathon.pdf` is an image-only event-page capture: text extraction returned no text and page 1 was visually inspected; its complete contents were not OCR-reviewed. The [official event page](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026) says the sprint has ended as checked on 2026-11-10; it does not establish whether this project was submitted. Other submission facts in project guides are dated and need fresh authoritative confirmation if relevant.

The prior audit locally reproduced malformed-attestation exceptions, the checker's normal return after an injected violation, negated-prose approval, and permissive output modes with umask 022. It ran 320 Python tests and five Node tests on the base with unclosed SQLite warnings. This planning session reran `python -m scripts.verify_submission` successfully against the current repository package; this checks public consistency, not private provider execution. No new model inference, cloud mutation, external scan or publication is part of planning.

Related-work records were checked at their primary abstracts, not fully reproduced: [AI Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942), [AgentRFC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23801), [Bounded Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15888), [secret collusion](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/861f7dad098aec1c3560fb7add468d41-Abstract-Conference.html), and [SQLite atomic commit](https://sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html). These motivate explicit adversary assumptions, composition tests, least authority and storage-failure boundaries; they do not validate this project. The author-hosted Byzantine paper and remaining reference records were consulted at metadata/abstract level where available; the Knight/Leveson DOI was unavailable in this check.
