# Roadmap

The baseline, historical study and controlled live-supervisor pilot are runnable
and documented. The [latest paper](report/latest.pdf) brings their findings
together; [current gates](GATES.md) separates completed evidence from remaining
work. This roadmap is a plan, not evidence of a new run or current cloud state.

## Delivered

- A dependency-free scripted lab with separate decision scoring, execution
  authorization, transactional effects and read-only replay.
- An institution kernel, bounded state exploration and mutation checks, plus
  corrected reconstruction of the retained two-model swarm study.
- A reviewed trusted-host model path with bounded transport, shared accounting,
  private receipts and single-use authorization. A fresh 160-request OpenRouter
  cohort completed after calibration; failed and stopped records remain separate.
- A latest report, public derived trial table and portable submission package.
- Dated NixOS/GCP worker and authenticated dashboard acceptance, and an executed
  Python/Node stable, upcoming, previous and retained-consumer CI matrix.

## Next work, in order of research value

1. **Investigate blanket denial.** The explicit presentation prevented invalid
   approvals by denying every case. Preserve controller enforcement and define a
   reviewed positive-evidence interface, then preregister a fresh held-out cohort.
   Measure valid-case acceptance alongside unsafe approval; retain this cohort
   unchanged. The [pilot record](experiments/2026-11-09-openrouter-paired-pilot.md)
   describes the proposed follow-up and its observation limits.
2. **Qualify additional provider paths.** Adaption access, billing bounds,
   applicable handling and per-request reconciliation need their own evidence.
   An OpenRouter result or owner-reported balance cannot supply it. Follow the
   [provider guide](ADAPTION.md) and [pilot runbook](CONTROLLED_PILOT.md).
3. **Complete infrastructure recovery and runtime migration.** Refresh cloud
   inventory, expiry evidence and costs before operating retained resources.
   Resolve remaining host/gateway, collector and rollback checks. Qualify a
   durable NixOS/Caddy update with the required runtime and recovery evidence;
   preserve the exact accepted rollback set.
4. **Finish the external entry.** The [submission guide](SUBMISSION.md) supplies
   the report, form copy and optional demo script. Actual form submission and its
   confirmation remain distinct from a merged repository or prepared ZIP.

## Separate future scope

Public observation, a global activity map, autonomous coding-worker dispatch,
stronger attestation, persisted-key/resume support and a Rust controller each need
an explicit design and applicable acceptance. No existing experiment establishes
third-party fleet identity or authorizes scanning, unsolicited contact, broader
spend or retirement of retained recovery assets.
